Job Tracker

A Chrome extension that saves job application details from posting pages into Google Spreadsheets you already own and control.

What it does

Open a job posting, open the Job Tracker side panel, review autofilled fields (company, role, URL, and more), and save or update a row in your sheet. Data is written only to spreadsheets you choose, using your Google account. Job Tracker does not sell your data or show ads.

How it works

  1. Install Job Tracker from the Chrome Web Store (or load it unpacked while developing).
  2. Sign in with Google and paste a spreadsheet link you can edit.
  3. On a job page, open the side panel, review the fields, and click Save.
