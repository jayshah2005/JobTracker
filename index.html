Job Tracker

Save job applications to Google Sheets

A Chrome extension that saves job application details from posting pages into Google Sheets you control.

What it does

Open a job posting, open the Job Tracker side panel, review autofilled fields (company, role, URL, and more), and save or update a row in your sheet. Job Tracker can only access sheets it creates or that you choose in Google Drive, and writes only to those, using your Google account. Job Tracker does not sell your data or show ads.

How it works

  1. Install Job Tracker from the Chrome Web Store (or load it unpacked while developing).
  2. Sign in with Google, then create a tracker sheet or choose an existing one from Google Drive.
  3. On a job page, open the side panel, review the fields, and click Save to tracker.
